# Healthcare Safety Specification

NaviMed V1 is a care-coordination and service-discovery product. It is not a diagnostic or prescribing system.

The product must not:

- diagnose a dental/medical condition;
- prescribe medication or doses;
- alter a treatment plan;
- guarantee emergency response;
- claim a pharmacy is open unless its published authoritative schedule says so;
- infer medical facts from appointment behavior.

Emergency contacts are public service information owned/approved by the administrator and authorized government sources. NaviMed does not claim to dispatch emergency services.

Pharmacy status must show freshness context; a stale schedule is not presented as current.
